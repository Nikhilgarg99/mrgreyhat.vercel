01 — About

About Me

I'm Deepak Rawat, an Information Security Engineer at OwnZap InfoSec and a penetration tester focused on helping businesses find and fix security gaps before attackers can exploit them.

My work is offensive-first: manual web, API, mobile and cloud assessments, real-world attack paths, and findings I can prove. I care less about how many issues a scanner reports and more about whether a bug is genuinely exploitable.

I also build open-source tooling to close the gaps scanners leave behind. Everything I deliver is aimed at one outcome: clear, prioritised fixes a developer can act on.

  • Manual-first testing, not just scanner noise
  • Web, API, mobile & cloud VAPT
  • Clear, prioritised, developer-friendly reports
  • Responsible disclosure & ethical tooling
